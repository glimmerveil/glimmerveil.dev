Shop terms

How buying a pack on glimmerveil.dev works.

Who you are buying from

Packs bought on this site are sold through Link, a service of Stripe. Link is the seller of record: it takes the payment, works out any sales tax or VAT, and emails your receipt. Its own terms are shown on the checkout page. Glimmerveil makes the packs and delivers them to you.

What you get

A pack is one file that you add to one of our free games. Most packs add a people to the game. Project Torch's is the full game: added to the free demo, it opens all of it. Buying a pack gives you a personal licence to use it in that game on your own devices. Please do not resell it or pass the file around. The games and the packs remain © Glimmerveil.

Getting your pack

After you pay you land on a page that gives you the file. That page's address gives you the file again for as long as we run this shop, unless the purchase is refunded or disputed. Anyone who has the address can get the file, so keep it to yourself, and keep your own copy of the file.

A pack is saved in the browser you add it to. Clearing that browser's data removes it, and you add the file again.

Prices and tax

Prices are in US dollars. Tax is calculated at checkout and depends on where you are. The checkout may show the price in your own currency, and it always shows the full total before you pay.

Refunds

Changed your mind? Write to support@glimmerveil.dev within 14 days of buying, or use the link on your receipt, and we refund you in full. After a refund the download page stops giving the file.

The small print

We work to keep the games and the packs running and we fix what breaks, but they are provided as they are, without a promise that they will work on every device and browser for ever. Nothing here takes away rights the law gives you where you live.
