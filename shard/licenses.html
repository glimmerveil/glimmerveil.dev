The ear (the Mic)

When you switch the Mic on, the page fetches Moonshine Tiny (Useful Sensors; MIT), as ONNX files from onnx-community/moonshine-tiny-ONNX (MIT). It hears you on your own device; nothing is sent anywhere.

Her voice (only when you switch it on)

The voice is Piper's libritts "high" voice (rhasspy/piper-voices), speaker p3922, fetched once from Hugging Face and run on your device. It is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (full text).

  • Credit: trained by the Piper project (rhasspy) on LibriTTS (Heiga Zen, Viet Dang, Rob Clark, Yu Zhang, Ron J.
