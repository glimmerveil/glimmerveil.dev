HEAT is the shield's: holding it up makes a little, stopping hits makes more, and heat from hits only bleeds off with the shield down (captains drop it for a moment to do that). Full heat collapses the shield for a few seconds; the guns fight on (their limit is power). Shield buffers soak a burst of hits; radiators shed heat; heat sinks hold more. Shields come in two kinds: directional (an arc that turns to the enemy) and the 360° bubble (every side, but each hit costs more heat). Full heat = overload: everything goes dark for seconds. Kinetic shots break shields, shells break armour, lances burn steadily. Every ship is built tile by tile: what you hit is what breaks.

POWER (shields, engines, beams, systems; not guns or pods): reactors feed every part on, or right beside, their conduits (paint them in the drydock with the Conduit brush; the roof-off view shows them, lit when live, red when cut). A conduit breaks when its tile is half wrecked, and the crew mend it with the tile. A part cut off from the reactors gets its power carried by the crew, a pack at a time: slower.

MISSILES: each hardpoint carries a pod. Swarm: six small agile ones a salvo. Heavy: three slow torpedoes. Hunter: one that does it all. After a salvo the next load comes down a feed line from an ammo rack and loads itself; off the lines, a crew member carries one up and stands at it while it loads. Guidance, warhead and booster modules touching a hardpoint make its missiles better.

CARGO AND CREW: holds carry plates, parts, food and trade goods. Crew patch damage to half with scrap, and past that fetch plates and parts from the holds. A breach drains a room; everyone has a suit with about a minute of air; crew seal breaches with a hull plate, and go out through an airlock to mend the outer hull. Crew eat food; hire more at markets to fill bunks.

DRONES AND DROIDS: uplinks give bandwidth. Each drone bay flies one type, rebuilt over time from the stores: light (gun drones), armoured (heavy plate, a heavier gun, slow), shield (their own shield that comes back; pairs of small missiles), lance (power drones with a beam). Gun drones load the ship's rounds in its kind, missile drones its missile loads; crystals linked to a lance bay and missile aux beside a shield bay boost them (DRONES button: out or home). Droid launchers send assault droids through the enemy hull. The crew WELD new marine landers in the troop bays and new droid pods in the launchers by hand, from hull plates and parts (BOARD button: welding).

MINING: mining lasers cut ore from asteroids, cargo beams pull loose ore and salvage into the holds (with no cargo beam its crew go out through an airlock and carry it in by the netful: lie within 50 of it; X sends the ship to it), a station's port beam carries what you buy from its cargo arm to your hold and what you sell back (it is yours when it is down your hatch), tractor beams hold a ship (more beams for bigger ships). On the map: Mine at an asteroid field, Land to send marines down to a world, Research to unlock parts. Factories and labs aboard work while you travel. Cloning vats bring the dead back for bio fluid.

MUNITIONS: guns fire rounds and pods fire missiles, kept in the holds and AMMO RACKS; they take no power (beams do). A rack sends crates and missiles down its FEED LINES to every gun and pod on them (paint them in the drydock, or Auto-feed); off the lines, or with a line cut, the crew carry them by hand. Five kinds of each: standard (iron + sulfur), advanced (+ exotic matter: armour stops half), EMP (silver: tears into shields, drains power), incendiary (more sulfur: sets fires), atomic (reactor fuel: a far bigger bang, and radiation). The ordnance works makes standard ones out on the map; the munitions plant (research) makes every kind. Rocks give iron, sulfur, silver, gold, atomic ore, crystals and exotic matter: gold makes chips, atomic ore makes reactor fuel, silver builds shields. A full rack that breaks goes up.

RAIDS AND WAR: a station of a faction at war with you fights where it hangs. Destroy it, or board it and hold its bridge, and its world is stripped: credits and supplies, its market shut for a while, its owner's hatred (a pirate den pays a bounty). Attacking a ship or station of a faction you're at peace with is war with them: the first order asks, the same order again gives it.

BEAM CRYSTALS: beams run on power and need no ammo, but crystals beside a beam (or linked to it through other crystals, four at most) change it: focus crystals reach further, amplifiers burn harder, a sulfur modulator sets fires, a silver one throws electric arcs that drain power and bite into shields, an atomic one irradiates the crew near the burn. Each crystal makes the beam draw 30% more power. Research: Beam optics, then Beam modulation.

READINESS (the campaign): every fight wears a ship down; supplies win it back day by day (a friendly port does it for free). Below 40% it's slow and its guns jam.

SENSORS: you only see what your fleet's sensors reach; the rest are fading contacts. Big ships are loud, and firing, the shield and the engines make any ship louder: hold fire and coast to creep in. A lock takes seconds (small ships take longer); sensor arrays reach further and lock faster. Jammers break locks held on their ship.
