Two new heroes from the forest

The Ranger carries a long bow: Multishot and Thorn Arrow from the start, and deeper in, Rain of Arrows, Moon Arrow and Starfall. The Druid carries a druid's staff: Living Vines, Bramble and Barkskin, and deeper in, Moonbeam, Eating Vines, Thorn Wall and Hurricane.

The Elves change the world's spells too

Once they're yours, the world starts blending in the moon, an element that blinds, and two new twists: Entangle, which roots whatever it hits and everything around it, and Seed, which sows brambles where it lands.

New gear, their own look, their own music

New gear: long and short bows, an elven blade, a druid's staff, a leaf circlet, a ranger's hood, an elven cloak, bark mail, bracers, soft boots and a moonstone. Long ears and a slighter build, a hand of living wood for your pointer that greens as a druid grows and blackens as a ranger does, and their own harp and flute music.
