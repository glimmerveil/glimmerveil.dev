Two heroes of the storm gods' people

The Stormcaller carries a storm staff: Lightning Strike, Gale and Storm's Blood, which hits harder and jolts your attackers still, and deeper in, Chain Lightning, Tornado and Thunderstorm. The Witch carries a bone wand: Hex, which leaves foes Weak, and a Mending Charm that gives life back every second, and deeper in, Evil Eye, Pestilence, Blood Rite, Ravens and Doom.

The Nords change the world's spells too

Once they're yours, the world starts blending in the curse, an element that leaves whatever it hits Weak, and two new twists: Thunderclap and Squall.

New gear, their own look, their own music

New gear: a storm staff, a bone wand, a horned helm, a raven hood, a fur mantle, a witch's shawl, fur boots, a raven charm and an arm ring. Tall, broad heroes with beards, braids and furs, a bare hand for your pointer that gathers butterflies as a stormcaller grows and black veins as a witch does, and their own music of horns and drones.
