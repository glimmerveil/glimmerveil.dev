Spells the world makes

Every spell is blended from pieces. An element: fire, cold, lightning, holy light, poison, stone, time, storm, blood and more. A way of travelling: a bolt, a nova, a rain. And a twist, like dragging foes toward it or turning them into sheep.

Every world rolls its own. Whatever you find is yours to learn.

Charge it. Hold it. Freeze them.

Hold a spell's key to charge it, up to twice as strong and twice the size, and a rain or a blizzard falls twice as long. Lightning keeps firing while you hold it. Cold builds until they freeze solid.

The priest raises the Sun's Shield, a dome that strengthens holy light and healing, and Deny Death ends in a healing pulse. The wizard's Ice Armour freezes whoever hits you and answers with a free Frost Nova.

Levels that never end

The world makes itself as you go: its acts, its levels, its monsters and its loot. Every fifth level has a town, and a time portal takes you back to any town you've reached.

Weather drifts through with the wind, day turns to night, and storms look like what they're made of. Fall three times on a level and the world makes itself again. You keep everything.

Two looks

Play it in full 3D, or press V for the text look: the whole world drawn in letters, lit by your spells. It's our favourite way to play.

The way an action RPG should play

A bag you can trust

A grid bag. Drag to move, double-click to wear, drag to the merchant to sell. Your gear shows on your hero.

Your hands, your keys

Left and right skills, Q and E, a bar of six, potions on F1 and F2. Drag skills onto the bar from the book.

Music that never repeats

The score is composed as you play, following where you are and how hard the fight is. Every hit has its sound.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. The whole game with the humans is free. The other peoples will come as packs.

What does it run on?

A computer with a recent browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Nothing to install. Your heroes stay in your browser, and you can back them up to a file from the title screen.

Does it collect anything?

No. No account, no trackers, no ads, and nothing ever comes back to us. The game runs on your machine. The one thing it ever fetches is an update for a pack you've added, from this site, and it sends nothing about you.

Is it made with AI?

Yes, all of it. Every line of the game's code was written by AI (Claude, the lead developer in our small studio's AI crew), directed by a human designer. There isn't a single picture or sound file in the game: the levels, the monsters, the spells, the music and every sound are made by code as you play.
