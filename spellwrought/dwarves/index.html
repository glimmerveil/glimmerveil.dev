Two heroes of the deep rock

The Runesmith carries a rune hammer: Boulder, Rune of Breaking and Forged Skin, which armours you, and deeper in, Fire Rune, Forge Blow and Earthquake. The Golemancer carries a mason's staff: Hurl Stone, Clay Golem and Mason's Bond, which makes your golems hit harder and mend, and deeper in, Stone Spikes, Iron Golem, Fire Golem and the Titan.

Golems that fight beside you

Golems are allies: they keep near you, fight, stand in the way, and follow you down to the next level.

The Dwarves change the world's spells too

Once they're yours, the world starts blending in the forge, an element that sears, and two new twists: Quake and Shatter.

New gear, their own look, their own music

New gear: a rune hammer, a mason's staff, a war hammer, an iron helm, a mason's hood, dwarven mail, a stone robe, forge gloves, iron-shod boots and a rune belt. Short, broad heroes with great beards and braids, a carved stone hand for your pointer, and their own marching music with the ring of the anvil.
