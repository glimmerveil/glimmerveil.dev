A town of violet twilight

Pour out the Mystics instead of plains folk and watch a town of violet twilight grow. Tall, pale and jewel-haired, they build towers of violet stone under slate spires with crystals at their peaks and glowing roses at their doors. A moonwell stands at the heart of every square, and their granary is a crystal tower.

They don't farm and they don't eat

They don't farm and they don't eat. They grow crystal instead of crops and live on its energy, they keep drifting crystal motes instead of cattle, and the cold never touches them. They sow their crystals right through the winter, and they fell trees and raise their towers by channelling.

Charm, glamour and sleep

In a war their enchanters charm, glamour and put the enemy to sleep, while the crystals over their heads fire beams of violet. Their magisters call the god's own powers down on whoever stands against them.

Your god gets time and space

The Time Bubble, which ages everyone caught inside. The Time Field, which stops time dead for every soldier in it. The Spatial Warp. Accelerated Decay, which ages people and buildings until they fall apart. And the Crystal Sentinel, with a beam of its own. Their god draws faith from their spells.

Play them in Sandbox, or pick them in Skirmish and fight the other gods.

How to add it

  1. Get the pack on its itch.io page. You download one file, arcane.godking.
  2. Open the free game, here on this site or on its itch page.
  3. Go to Saves › Packs › Add a pack… and pick the file.

It stays in that browser. On an iPhone, save the file to Files first. If you play in both places, add it in each. You can download it again any time from your itch library.

Updates come in on their own. Fixes and new things for the Arcane arrive by themselves: the game checks glimmerveil.dev for the pack's newest version (a plain download, nothing about you is sent) and it's in from the next time you play.
