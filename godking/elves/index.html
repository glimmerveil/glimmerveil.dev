Homes grown, not built

Pour out elves instead of plains folk and watch a different people grow a town. Their homes are grown, not built: woven huts, round houses under leaf roofs, oval halls, and houses up in great trees. Their temple is a Grove. They store food in willow and hive granaries, meet in the moon-hall, and keep watch from the trees.

Foragers and hunters

They forage instead of farming wheat, hunt elk instead of keeping cows, and grow gourd vines up the trees round their towns. Their god keeps the woods around them grown. Light on their feet, short on stone, and quick to quarrel.

The best archers in the game

They're the best archers in the game. Their rangers go unseen in the woods with great bows and two-handed swords. Their druids march with the army and sing roots into an enemy's wall until it comes down.

Your god gets the wild

Bramble and Living Vines that crawl along the ground and coil round whoever they catch. Eating Vines that pass from one living thing to the next. The Thornwall, and at its biggest a briar with roses. The Treant, woken from a tree to guard the town and rooted back down when it's done. And the Thorn Elemental against a great army. Their god's hand is living wood: thorns and blackened bark for a cruel god, leaves and blossom for a kind one.

Play them in Sandbox, or pick them in Skirmish and fight the other gods.
