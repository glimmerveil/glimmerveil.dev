Longhouses, mead halls and longships

Pour out Nords instead of plains folk and watch a different people build. They raise bow-sided longhouses under great thatched roofs, a stave Hof with dragons on every gable, and mead halls where they drink to their dead. They keep pigs instead of cows, pen them at the edge of town and salt them down for winter. They grow barley instead of wheat. Their god-priests, the godi, call down the storm. And when there's timber to spare, they build longships.

The hardest people in a fight

They're the hardest people in a fight, and harder still in a storm. One footman in five is a berserker, and their veterans become champions. Half their bowmen ride as horse archers. Their witches follow the army and curse whoever stands against it, halving their blows and making them sick.

Your god gets the storm

Lightning, Storm and Tornado, plus two powers of their own: Thor's Hammer, one great bolt that shatters what it hits and leaps on to the enemies around it, and Gale, a howling wind that throws people off their feet and tears down timber walls. Their god's hand is bare. A cruel god grows black veins up the back of it. A kind one gets glowing storm-blue butterflies.

Play them in Sandbox, or pick them in Skirmish and fight the other gods.

How to add it

  1. Get the pack on its itch.io page. You download one file, nords.godking.
  2. Open the free game, here on this site or on its itch page.
  3. Go to Saves › Packs › Add a pack… and pick the file.

It stays in that browser. On an iPhone, save the file to Files first. If you play in both places, add it in each. You can download it again any time from your itch library.

Updates come in on their own. Fixes and new things for the Nords arrive by themselves: the game checks glimmerveil.dev for the pack's newest version (a plain download, nothing about you is sent) and it's in from the next time you play.
